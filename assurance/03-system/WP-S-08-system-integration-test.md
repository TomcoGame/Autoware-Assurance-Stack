## 1. Purpose

How the item is integrated, from the safety monitor alone to the item in the vehicle, and how each step is tested. The tests show that the technical safety requirements of [WP-S-02](WP-S-02-technical-safety-requirements.md), the interfaces of [WP-S-05](WP-S-05-hsi-specification.md) and the cybersecurity requirements of [WP-S-07](WP-S-07-cybersecurity-requirements-architecture.md) hold once their parts are put together (ISO 26262-4 §7). Verification of the integrated system against its requirements as a whole is [WP-S-09](WP-S-09-system-verification.md). Validation in the ODD is in 06-validation.

Needed at gate G4 ([WP-M-00](../01-management/WP-M-00-work-product-register.md)). This version is the strategy and specification. Results follow when the parts exist.

The model is kept in `integ/integ.yaml`; `integ/integ.py` checks it and writes the tables and Figure 2-1. It refuses a model in which:

- a component is integrated twice or never, or an interface is exercised before both its ends exist;
- a case runs before everything it covers is integrated;
- a requirement, signal or cybersecurity requirement is covered by no case;
- a requirement with a time budget is never timed;
- a fault-detecting requirement is never tested by fault injection.

## 2. Integration strategy and levels

The item is integrated from the inside out, in four steps:

1. **The safety monitor, alone.** It is the one element every rated requirement passes through, so it is integrated and tested first, with everything around it simulated at its pins.
2. **The safety channel.** Its sensors and processor are added next, then their report to the monitor.
3. **The item on the bench.** The Autoware computer and a remote operations station join, with the vehicle still simulated behind its real bus interfaces.
4. **The item in the vehicle.** Only the last step needs the vehicle, which is chosen at G2 (DQ-008).

Faults that would be dangerous on the road are injected on the bench first, and on the vehicle only on a closed track.

<!-- END GENERATED: integ-environments -->

**Simulation takes credit only where it has been checked.** A result from software in the loop counts as evidence only where WP-V-08 §3 has checked the simulator against the real vehicle (DQ-036 A). Elsewhere it is for development and regression, and the case is repeated on the bench or the track.

**Fault injection on the vehicle waits on a decision.** The faults of IT-13, IT-14 and IT-17 matter most at the top of the speed range. Whether they may be injected on the real vehicle up to 50 km/h is put to the lead (DQ-038, recommended: yes, on a closed track with an independent stop and a test safety plan). Until then ENV-4 is planned but not bounded.

## 4. Test specification

Each case names the step and environment it runs at, its methods, what it covers, the time budgets of the requirements it covers (read from WP-S-04, not typed), and its pass criterion. The ASIL of a case is the highest of the requirements it covers. It sets the rigour of the test and of its independent review (WP-M-02).

## 5. Results

No test has been run. Results are recorded per case, against a released configuration (WP-P-01), with the measured times where a case has a budget, and each failure is a problem report (WP-P-03).
