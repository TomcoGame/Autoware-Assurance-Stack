## 1. Purpose

Cybersecurity goals, claims and the concept that meets them. The TARA ([WP-C-09](WP-C-09-tara.md)) rated sixteen threat scenarios, and the lead decided that every risk of 3 or more is reduced (DQ-027 A). A threat whose risk is reduced gets a cybersecurity goal; one whose risk is retained gets a cybersecurity claim, a written reason why. The concept states the requirements that meet the goals, on the item and on its operational environment (the remote operations centre and the fleet and update backends).

Needed at gate G1 ([WP-M-00](../01-management/WP-M-00-work-product-register.md)).

The cybersecurity assurance level (CAL) of a goal sets how rigorously its requirements are developed and verified. It is taken, as ISO/SAE 21434 Annex E suggests, from the impact of the goal's threats and their attack vector: how close an attacker must be (physical access, local, adjacent such as radio range, or network). The attack vectors are restricted ([WP-M-09 §4](../01-management/WP-M-09-cybersecurity-plan.md#4-handling-of-cybersecurity-information)); the CALs they give are recorded in `cybersecurity/cybersecurity.yaml`, and the CAL table must be checked against the standard by the reviewer (OI-1).

**Safety and cybersecurity meet in CG-01.** Remote commands are already bounded by the safety monitor (FSR-08.02), so an attacker who forges one cannot drive the vehicle outside the envelope. CG-01 closes what the envelope cannot see: a release while passengers get out, a route change, and commands that are inside the envelope but not the operator's. Its requirement CSR-01 ends at the safety monitor, the rated element, not at Autoware.

### 4.3 How the concept fits the safety architecture

- **Rated elements stay small.** The cryptographic check of CSR-01 runs in the safety monitor, so the monitor's software grows; its ASIL D development (WP-W-*) must cover that code, and its timing must fit the detection times of WP-S-04.
- **Availability is a safety property here.** CG-02 is met by a second link (CSR-03) and by the vehicle's ability to reach a minimal risk condition without any link (AOU-09, WP-C-12), not by trying to make jamming impossible.
- **Updates.** CSR-05 is the same rule as STPA SR-03: updates only out of service, versions checked as a set; it serves safety and security at once.
