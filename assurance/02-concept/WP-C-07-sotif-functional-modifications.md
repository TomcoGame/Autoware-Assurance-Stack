## 1. Purpose

Changes to the function, its ODD or its architecture that reduce SOTIF risk to the acceptance criteria. ISO 21448 §8 asks for such modifications for every hazard whose risk is not yet acceptable. Here none is shown acceptable yet, so every insufficiency of [WP-C-06](WP-C-06-sotif-insufficiencies-triggering-conditions.md) gets at least one. With no driver, "hand the task back" is never one of them: each modification changes what the item does, where it is used, how it is built or how it is operated.

Needed at gate G1 ([WP-M-00](../01-management/WP-M-00-work-product-register.md)).

20 functional modifications address the 18 insufficiencies of WP-C-06: 10 already required by the concept (a requirement, ODD monitor or assumption) though not yet built, 8 new work, 2 waiting on DQ-025, DQ-026. Every insufficiency is addressed; none is shown to be enough yet (WP-V-03).

The modifications are kept in `modifications/modifications.yaml`; `modifications/modifications.py` works out the hazards each one addresses through WP-C-06 and writes the tables and Figure 2-1. It refuses a model in which an insufficiency has no modification, or a gap in the specification is answered only with data or architecture: a function that was never specified to do something is not fixed by more training data or a second channel alone.

**The safety channel carries the most.** FM-02, the independent safety monitor and its own sensors (DQ-002 A, DQ-018 A), addresses five performance insufficiencies at once. It makes the item tolerant of Autoware's perception and prediction errors, but not of conditions that blind both channels (WP-C-06 §4): those rest on FM-10 and on the dependent failure analysis (WP-A-03).

**Most new work is in Autoware.** FM-03, FM-04, FM-06, FM-09, FM-15, FM-16 and FM-19 change or add Autoware packages; they are developed in WP-W-03 under the same configuration management as the pinned code, and each is QM (DQ-002 A): their job is to make the monitor's interventions rare, not to carry a safety goal.

## 3. ODD restrictions

No modification restricts the ODD further. The analysis does not show a condition inside the ODD that only a restriction can answer, and the lead has set the ODD's limits (DQ-001 B, DQ-011 C, DQ-012 A).

One modification lowers exposure inside the ODD instead (FM-08): route planning prefers routes without unprotected left turns across more than one oncoming lane and without multi-lane roundabouts, when one of similar length exists. Both remain inside the ODD and are still validated. This is a reversible routing choice, taken by Claude as DQ-024 (assumed, 2026-10-23); the lead can overrule it.

If the evaluation of WP-V-03 finds a condition the item cannot handle acceptably, the restriction goes back to the lead as an ODD decision, and WP-C-02 changes with it.
